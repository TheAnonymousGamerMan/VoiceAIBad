Reference sentences for collect_data.py
========================================
Read these aloud naturally while recording (press key -> start, say the
sentence, press key -> stop, then type it in). Mix in your own sentences
too -- variety matters more than sticking to this exact list.

Set 1 - everyday
1. I need to grab my keys before we leave.
2. The coffee is still too hot to drink.
3. Turn left at the next intersection.
4. My phone battery is almost dead again.
5. Can you pass me the salt, please?
6. We should leave in about ten minutes.
7. The dog wants to go for a walk.
8. I forgot to charge my laptop overnight.
9. It's supposed to rain later this afternoon.
10. Let's order pizza for dinner tonight.
